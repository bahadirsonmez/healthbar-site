Optional AI with Master Ronnie

A gentle guide, only with your permission.

Get an AI overview of selected wellness patterns, and chat with Master Ronnie about habits and progress. HealthBar processes your HealthKit records on your device and shares only selected, derived findings for AI responses—never your raw history.

  • Review what is shared, and manage AI consent and saved conversations in Settings.
  • No harsh scores or impossible streaks—just manageable next steps.
  • HealthBar+ adds deeper insights, longer-term patterns, more AI chat, and an ad-free experience.
